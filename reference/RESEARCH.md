# Paw and Friends: what makes @led3.an and the low-poly cat niche work

Research run 2026-11-21. Validated ledger: `run/` (53 sources, 35 verbatim quotes, 27 claims, `validate_run` passed). Rendered brief: `run/brief.md`. Raw data: `posts.json`.
Citations **[S#]** use the source numbers in `run/brief.md`. Lines marked *(agent analysis)* are my reading of covers or numbers, not something a source says. Lines marked **UNVERIFIED** could not be checked.

How the data was collected: TikTok metadata via `yt-dlp --skip-download` (no video files), comments via TikTok's public comment endpoint (first 50 per video, sorted by likes), follower counts from TikTok profile pages in the browser, and cover images viewed. Bright Data returned "Customer is not active" for every call, and the engine's own TikTok fetch could not start (patchright Chromium is not installed). So the data above was written into the ledger as sources by the agent. All TikTok sources share one domain, so almost every claim is marked single-source.

## Key findings

1. **The format is a cat couple with the caption "us?".** @led3.an has only ~32K followers [S1], but 5 posts passed 900K views and the best reached 2.1M (39.8x the 52,800 median) [S2]. Its 41 "us?" posts have a median of 131,200 views, against 16,200 for the 35 other posts. Their share rate is 7.3% of views, against 4.5% [S2]. The video is made to be **sent to a partner**: the top comment on the 2.1M post (21,610 likes) is about sending it to a situationship, followed by "Me and my bf" [S3].
2. **Shorter is better.** Posts of 9 s or less have a median of 110,000 views, 10–15 s posts 33,800 and posts over 15 s 22,900. The median length is 11 s, and every post over 900K views is 7–13 s long [S2].
3. **It has to be the same cast, in the same world.** A ginger cat and a black cat share one bedroom set: red sheet, leaf wallpaper, bare bulb. 5 of the 8 most-viewed covers show this pair, and 5 of the 8 are in the bed set *(agent analysis of covers)* [S2]. The flops are off-brand: a 2D Windows-XP cat, a crab and a flat 2D fish cat sit at 0.06–0.07x the median [S2].
4. **Cozy beats parody.** Early game parodies worked once (Skyrim 281K) but mostly sat low. After the switch to cozy couple loops, the 2026 median is 107,900 against 24,000 in 2025 [S2].
5. **The hits use quiet, original sound.** 62 of 76 posts use an original sound [S2], and viewers praise the sounds [S8]. **UNVERIFIED:** what is actually in the audio (meows, ambience, music), because the videos were not played.
6. **The schedule is steady.** The median gap between posts is 3 days, about 2 posts a week, with one 55-day break [S2]. The top 10 posts hold 65% of all views [S2], so a hit is rare and needs a steady flow of posts.
7. **Viewers want it hand-made.** Comments such as "This is better than AI" (603 likes) and "thank you for not using gen AI" (518) appear [S5][S4], and a vossadova viewer asks whether Gen AI was used [S6]. The craft itself is part of the appeal.
8. **Viewers see themselves or their own cats in it.** The top comment on the 1.3M post (8,454 likes) is a photo reply, "They are literally my cats" [S4]. On ekzentriko's pets-to-PS1 series, the top comments are people posting their own pets [S13].
9. **Videos the viewer can feel spread hugely.** On airdays' fan video (19.7M views), the top comment (116,544 likes) reads "why am i lowk feeling a breeze" [S11][S12].
10. **The niche is mainly a TikTok niche.** airdays has 19.7M views on TikTok but ~84K on its best recent YouTube Short. The Led3an YouTube channel peaks at ~26K [S33][S34][S12]. The YouTube channel is assumed to be the same creator, which is **UNVERIFIED**.

## 4. Visual style breakdown

This section comes from the agent viewing 13 led3.an covers and 8 peer covers. It is *agent analysis* and not frame-exact.
- **Resolution and pixels.** The frame is rendered small and scaled up. Texture texels are visibly blocky, and the image has a dithered, slightly noisy grain. The posts are 540–1080 px wide and in mixed aspect ratios [S2]. A viewer noticed the vertex jitter ("random vertex warping on the static objects") *(posts.json comments, 1.3M post)*.
- **Textures.** Photo-real cat fur is projected onto faceted low-poly bodies, with sharp triangle ears and pixel whiskers. The cats look like real cats, not cartoons. The flat 2D or toon posts flopped.
- **Lighting.** There is one warm practical light (a lamp or a bare bulb) with strong bloom and a blown-out glow. Night scenes use a blue room with a warm screen or lamp glow. Pink and red overexposed washes are used for sleepy scenes.
- **Grading and effects.** Warm and saturated, with soft vignetting and rounded, CRT-like edges on some frames. There is a faint scanline or dot pattern, but no heavy chromatic aberration was visible on the covers (**UNVERIFIED** in motion).
- **Camera.** Mostly static or slow, level with the bed, or a high 3/4 angle into a small room, or top-down onto a beach towel. **UNVERIFIED**: whether the camera moves.
- **Scenes.** Bed and bedroom (most of the hits), kitchen with food (crêpes, ramen, mochi), beach, hammock, clothesline, campfire.
- **Text.** No overlay on the hit covers. The caption carries the joke.
- **Sound.** Mostly original sounds (62/76) [S2]. **UNVERIFIED**: which meows, ambience or music.
- **Peers for contrast.** airdays and zinancho use a clean white or flat background with one cat. vossadova uses warm golden light with depth of field. ekzentriko shows a Blender UI with the pet photo.

## 5. Recommendations for Paw and Friends

**Format rules taken from the evidence:**
- **Length:** 7–9 s, one beat, looping. Do not go over 13 s [S2].
- **Hook:** frame 1 is already the cute pose. Use no text overlay, and put a relationship caption in the description: "us?", "us on <holiday>?", "me and my sister?" [S2][S3].
- **Sound:** one quiet original bed of room tone with a soft meow or purr, and no narrator in this format. The niche's hits are wordless [S2]. Your storyteller format can stay as a separate, longer series.
- **Cast and set:** fix one home set (the Paw and Friends living room or bed) and reuse it. Off-brand topics flop [S2].
- **Cadence:** 2–3 posts a week, on TikTok first, then cross-posted [S2][S33].
- **Craft:** viewers reward visible hand-made work [S4][S5]. An occasional "real cat vs low-poly" reveal plays to both this and the "my cats" reaction [S4][S13].

**10 strongest video ideas** (each is *agent analysis* grounded in the cited pattern):
1. **"us?": Paw and Silent asleep in one bed under a lamp** (7 s). This copies the pattern of the 2.1M, 1.3M and 928K posts [S2][S3].
2. **"us at 3am?": Paw grooms Silent, Silent gives up and flops.** A couple dynamic built to be sent to a partner [S3].
3. **"me and my sister?": Yeji and Paw fighting over the one sunny spot.** It reuses "us?" for siblings and friends, which widens who it gets sent to *(agent analysis)* [S3].
4. **"us making breakfast?": Zhenya and Paw at the stove (crêpes or eggs).** Kitchen and food scenes reached 446K, 145K and 130K [S2].
5. **"grandma visiting?": Babushka Lusya on the bed, the others slowly scooting away.** This gives the angry-granny character a relatable family joke *(agent analysis)*.
6. **Franko belly-up among snack bags ("already ate everything...").** This is the 1.1M kitten-beach beat [S2].
7. **Franko and a butterfly in the dark, "Kitty nooo" suspense** (7 s). It copies the 968K post [S2].
8. **"time to cool off": a cat leans into a fan, wind in the fur, facing the camera.** This is the 19.7M sensory-illusion beat from airdays [S11][S12].
9. **"us on <holiday>?" holiday cuddle loops** (Christmas tree or New Year for the next post). Holiday "us?" posts from led3.an and airdays performed: Easter 110K and 11.1M [S2][S12].
10. **"Real cat → PS1 cat" reveal: a photo of Silent morphs into the low-poly Silent** (8–10 s). It draws on ekzentriko's pets-to-PS1 series (610K) and the "they are literally my cats" reaction [S20][S4]. Invite viewers to post their own cats.

**Avoid:** 2D or flat posts, non-cat subjects, clips over 15 s and space or abstract sketches. Each of these underperformed [S2].

## What could not be verified
- The **videos were not played**, so hooks, camera moves, the audio content and any text inside the videos are inferred from covers and captions only.
- **Bright Data** was inactive ("Customer is not active"), and the engine's TikTok lane needs a browser install, so the data was collected with yt-dlp, the comment endpoint and browser page text instead. Source timestamps in the ledger are set to 22:10Z for the batch (actual retrieval was 22:08–22:20Z).
- The **latest led3.an posts** (after 2026-09-25) may be missing, because yt-dlp returned 76 entries.
- **Watch time, retention and audience geography** are not public.
- The YouTube @led3an channel is **assumed** to be the same creator.
- The TikTok related-videos endpoint failed, so peers were found from the #lowpolycat tag page and web search, which is not a complete census.
