# Format and ideas (from the niche research)

Evidence: `cats_setup/reference/RESEARCH.md`. 76 @led3.an posts were scored against the account's median (52,800 views), alongside 10 peer accounts.

## The format
- **The beat is "us?".** Two cats in a couple, sibling or friend moment that a viewer sends to someone. On @led3.an, "us?" posts have a median of 131K views against 16K for everything else, and are shared 7.3% vs 4.5%. Variants: "us on <holiday>?", "me and my sister?", "us at 3am?".
- **Length is 7–9 s:** one beat that loops seamlessly. Never go over 13 s; posts over 15 s sit at ~0.4x the median.
- **Hook:** frame 1 is already the cute pose. No build-up and no on-screen text. The caption (in the description) carries the joke.
- **Sound is wordless:** room tone, purrs, soft meows, chewing, a fan, rain. 62 of 76 led3.an posts use an original sound.
- **Cast:** the same cats, the same home. Off-brand posts (2D, non-cats, abstract) flop at 0.06–0.4x.
- **Cadence:** 2–3 posts a week, TikTok first, YouTube Shorts as well. Hits are rare (the top 10 posts make 65% of views), so the stream matters.
- **Look:** the format is led3.an's, but the picture is our smooth Dive Cats 1 render (STYLE.md). led3.an's pixel look was tried and judged too pixelated.
- **Craft shows:** viewers praise "better than AI". Answer the AI question No; a real-cat → PS1 reveal now and then plays to this.
