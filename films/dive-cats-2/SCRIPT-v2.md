# Dive Cats 2: "Fishing" (v2, one cat at a time)

The sequel to Dive Cats. After the jump, the divers go spear-fishing. We see each cat on its own, in its own spot on the reef, catching its own kind of fish, and each fish is bigger than the last. Little Franko aims at a shark; the shark turns around and looks at him. That is the hook for the next episode.

- **Length:** about 23.5 s, vertical 9:16, 30 fps, final in 4K.
- **Look:** three.js, low-poly PS1 in the led3.an style, on the reef approved in v1.
- **Voice:** Bill (calm storyteller), the same as episode 1.
- **Music:** Kevin MacLeod, "Island Meet and Greet" (CC BY). It stops dead when the shark turns.
- **Gear:** the same masks and toy spearguns. The red suction-cup tip boops the fish and sticks; nothing is sharp.

## One catch (about 2.8 s per cat)

The cat swims in and aims. The fish crosses in front. Pop: the spear flies on its line. Boing: the cup sticks to the fish. The cat reels it in, turns to us and hugs it. A happy cat sound and bubble hearts. Then a hard cut to the next cat.
